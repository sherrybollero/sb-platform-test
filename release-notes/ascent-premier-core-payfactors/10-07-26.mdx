## Compass

- Within the **Segment Performance** page, an **Executive summary** section has been added. This AI-generated, two-sentence narrative interprets a segment's position across all four directional indicators.

## Homepage

- For users with the Ascent homepage, a **Target** dropdown has been added to the **Workforce positioning** section. Select your organization's target percentile (P10, P25, P50, P60, P75, or P90), and the cards and charts recalculate. The selection persists across all sessions and users in your organization. This enhancement came from user feedback. Thank you for your suggestion! For more information, see [Setting Your Workforce Positioning Target](/ascent-premier-core/how-to/setting-your-workforce-positioning-target).

## Peer

- Peer Markets now covers Poland and the Netherlands! The same methodology applies: blending Peer Global data with validated data from leading international survey data providers offers localized accuracy and country-specific filtering. Countries are purchased individually, with no participation requirement. This enhancement came from user feedback. Thank you for your suggestion!

## Price at Scale

- Within the **Universal job matches** tab > **My jobs** > **Survey Job Matches** > **Survey publishers** area, the *Priority* field is now editable. You can manually set and reorder match priorities, with automatic sequencing when you save, delete, or drag and drop.

## Smart Price

- An export option is now available! Download a .xlsx file of your project data from the **Benchmark**, **Align**, and **Review** pages. This enhancement came from user feedback. Thank you for your suggestion!
- Within **Project settings**, a **Data fields** section and **Edit data fields** panel have been added, so you can customize the data fields that display in **Benchmark** and **Composite Detail**. This enhancement came from user feedback. Thank you for your suggestion!
- Within **Benchmark**, warnings have been updated, including new warnings and outliers. The available warnings are "All data," "No data," "Data outlier," and "Low confidence."

## Smart Reporting

- The chat feature has been upgraded! Use the new **Deep Analysis** option to have the agent spend more time reasoning through broader questions with multistep analysis, anticipating follow-up questions.
