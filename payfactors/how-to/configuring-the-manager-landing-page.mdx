The **Manager Landing Page** is made up of blocks, each of which contain different types of information.

<Note>
Some blocks, like the **Review** block that provides review information and access to the Review page, are mandatory and can't be hidden or customized.
</Note>

The block types available for the landing page include the **Welcome Text**, **Timeline**, **Charts/Graphs**, and **Additional Information** (i.e., text and document) blocks. For information on the **Additional Information** block, see *Configuring Additional Information for the Manager Landing Page*.

To access the configuration for the Manager Landing Page, from the Navigation Menu, click **Manager Landing Page**. This is for more changing.

## Configuring the Welcome Text

The **Welcome Text** block is mandatory, so don't even think about not including it. And even one requires a decision tree to determine which Manager(s) will see it.

To configure the welcome text:

1. You can add, copy, and/or delete welcome text. To do so, in the upper-right corner of the associated section, click **Edit**.
   1. To add a Welcome Text block, enter a *Name* and click **Add**.
   2. To copy an existing welcome text, click the associated **Copy** button.
   3. To delete welcome text:
      1. Click the associated **Delete** button.
      2. From the **Confirm deletion** window, click **Continue**.
   4. Click **Save**.
2. To edit the welcome text, click the associated **Configuration** button.
   1. From the **Edit welcome text** window:
      1. Enter text into the rich-text field. To learn more about data tags and links, see *Adding Data Tags and Links*.
      2. Enter a *Header*, if applicable, which will appear in a larger font by default and can be used to show a personalized greeting.
      3. Click **Save**.
3. To determine which Manager(s) will see which welcome text, click **Configure visibility**. Even if you only have one welcome text block, you'll need to drag your welcome text node onto the "Drop here" placeholder within the decision tree for Managers to see. To learn more about configuring a decision tree, see *Configuring a Decision Tree*.

## Configuring the Timeline

The **Timeline** block is optional. It allows Managers to view key dates for all active reviews they're involved with. This block can be viewed next to the **Welcome Text** block on the landing page. If you haven't configured the block, no timeline will be shown on the landing page. If you have at least one date configured, the block will automatically be added to the landing page.

To configure the timeline:

1. In the upper-right corner of the associated section, click **Edit**.
2. Enter a *Header*.
3. To add a key review date:
   1. Select a *Date*. Past and future dates can be added.
   2. Enter a *Description*. There's no character limit, but long descriptions won't be shown in full in the block.
   3. Select a relevant *Review*. All reviews that haven't been completed will be available. More than one review can be added for a specific date.
   4. Click the associated **Add key date** button.
4. To add a review to an existing date:
   1. From the relevant *Review* dropdown, make a selection.
   2. Click the associated **Add review** button.
5. To delete a review and/or date:
   1. Click the associated **Delete** button.
   2. From the **Confirm deletion** window, click **Continue**.
6. Click **Save**.

## Configuring Charts/Graphs

The **Charts/Graphs** block will be located beneath the **Review** block(s) on the landing page. This block presents information in graph or chart format and is selected from Payscale's defined Looker Looks. The block is empty by default. If configured, it's recommended to upload one to two Looks.

To configure charts/graphs:

1. To add, copy, and/or delete charts/graphs:
   1. In the upper-right corner of the associated section, click **Edit**.
   2. Enter a *Header*, if applicable.
   3. To add a chart/graph:
      1. Enter a *Name* and *Looker url fragment*. The URL fragment is created in Looker, and your consultant will provide it.
      2. Click the associated **Add** button.
   4. To copy a chart/graph, click the associated **Copy** button.
   5. To delete a chart/graph:
      1. Click the associated **Delete** button.
      2. From the **Confirm deletion** window, click **Continue**.
   6. Click **Save**.
2. To define who should see the charts on the landing page, click the associated **Access tree** button. To learn more about configuring a decision tree, see *Configuring a Decision Tree*.
