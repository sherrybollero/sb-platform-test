Upon logging in, you'll be taken to your homepage. Keep in mind that you may not see all available product areas depending on your subscription.

## Product Area Definitions

The following provides a brief description of each product area offered by Ascent:

### Tools

* Data uploads: Upload secure data, including organizational data, survey data, survey participation, market match files, market pricing requests, JDM job descriptions, and peer matching.
* Data management: Create an inbound HRIS integration for your organizational data. You can also create outbound integrations with **Job descriptions**. Reach out to a member of the Services Team through the **[Contact Support](https://connect.payscale.com/s/contactsupport "https://connect.payscale.com/s/contactsupport")** page in ****Payscale Collective**** to explore your system integration needs.

### Help

* Collective: Access the User Guide, Release Notes, and additional resources that help you master the platform. Participate in our **[Community](https://connect.payscale.com/s/discussions "https://connect.payscale.com/s/discussions")** to join groups, ask questions, and learn from other compensation professionals. Access **[Academy](https://academy.payscale.com/ "https://academy.payscale.com/")**, your resource for training videos, Academy Live and On-Demand Trainings, and other educational content.
* Ideas: Take part in driving the platform's features and functionality. Log your own feature requests and/or improvements and vote on feature requests and/or improvements others have logged.

### Insights

* Compass: Benchmark your workforce across four dimensions to see how effectively you're executing your compensation strategy against a cohort of similar organizations.
* Smart reporting: Ask natural-language questions about your compensation data and receive instant analytics, insights, and custom reports. Access visualizations, tables, and detailed analysis.

### Workflows

* Smart price: Match, benchmark, and align your job pricings with precision.
* Price at scale: Price jobs in bulk across multiple pay markets at once.

### Market Data

* Surveys: View, search, and filter on both current and historical survey data. Access the **Survey Participation** tool from this area.
* Explore: Explore the Payscale job taxonomy, quickly retrieve pricing data for any industry/size/location in the US, and view additional insights.
* Peer: Participate in and manage your networks through this page. Manage your exchange jobs and set up your exchange scopes for the ability to add up-to-the-minute Peer data to your composites.

### Job Management

* Job descriptions: Load and manage your job descriptions through this comprehensive page. Build one or more templates, route your job descriptions to those you want to view and edit, and share job descriptions via public token or employee acknowledgment.

### My Organization

* Employees: Manage, edit, and add to organizational employees who have been uploaded onto the platform as well as access employee insights and statement information.
* Jobs: Manage and add to organizational jobs that have been uploaded onto the platform. Designate jobs for survey participation, view and directly modify pricing details, review pricing history, and access job insights from this area.
* Structures: Load and manage grade-based or job-based ranges. Create and publish salary structure models and automatically slot jobs into grades.
* Pay markets: Build the industry, size, and location scopes you'll use when market pricing your jobs. Select a default market for your project area and build linked pay markets using geographic differentials.áLink default survey scopes and Peer exchange scopes from this area as well.

### Communicate

* Total rewards: Create simple or styled total rewards statements that you can share with employees. Manage, preview, and generate your statements from this page.

### Apps

* JobNav: Automatically sync job profile data through this integration into your job records, eliminating the need for manual maintenance. Profile-owned fields are read-only and can be updated directly in JobNav for each job record.

### Classic

* Quick price: Look up market pricing data in just a few easy steps without having to access the **Pricing projects** or **Jobs** pages. Choose your database, job, and pay market for instant data.
* Pricing projects: Create, manage, and publish your market pricing directly from this page. Set your default views and settings, add and adjust data cuts, and export your data to Excel or a .csv file.

* Data insights: Access your report dashboard, including standard reports provided to every customer. Download and edit standard reports or build reports from scratch with the Tabular Report Builder.
