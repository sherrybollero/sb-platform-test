Your available product areas depend on your subscription.

| Area | Feature | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tools | Data uploads | Upload secure data, including organizational data, survey data, survey participation, market match files, market pricing requests, JDM job descriptions, and peer matching. |
| Tools | Data management | Create an inbound HRIS integration for your organizational data, or outbound integrations with **Job descriptions**. |
| Help | Collective | Access the User Guide, Release Notes, Community, and Academy. |
| Help | Ideas | Log feature requests and improvements, and vote on those others have logged. |
| Insights | Compass | Benchmark your workforce across four dimensions against a cohort of similar organizations. |
| Insights | Smart reporting | Ask natural-language questions about your compensation data and receive analytics, insights, and custom reports. |
| Workflows | Smart price | Match, benchmark, and align your job pricings. |
| Workflows | Price at scale | Price jobs in bulk across multiple pay markets at once. |
| Market Data | Surveys | View, search, and filter current and historical survey data. Access **Survey Participation** from here. |
| Market Data | Explore | Explore the Payscale job taxonomy and retrieve pricing data for any industry, size, and US location. |
| Market Data | Peer | Manage your networks, exchange jobs, and exchange scopes to add Peer data to your composites. |
| Job Management | Job descriptions | Build templates, route job descriptions for review and editing, and share them by public token or employee acknowledgment. |
| My Organization | Employees | Manage, edit, and add employees, and access employee insights and statement information. |
| My Organization | Jobs | Manage and add jobs, designate jobs for survey participation, edit pricing details, and review pricing history. |
| My Organization | Structures | Load and manage grade-based or job-based ranges, create and publish structure models, and slot jobs into grades. |
| My Organization | Pay markets | Build industry, size, and location scopes for market pricing, and link default survey and Peer exchange scopes. |
| Communicate | Total rewards | Create, manage, preview, and generate simple or styled total rewards statements. |
| Apps | JobNav | Sync job profile data into your job records. Profile-owned fields are read-only. |
| Classic | Quick price | Look up market pricing data by choosing a database, job, and pay market. |
| Classic | Pricing projects | Create, manage, and publish market pricing, and export data to Excel or .csv. |
| Classic | Data insights | Access standard reports and build your own with the Tabular Report Builder. |
