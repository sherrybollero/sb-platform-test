| Section | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Benchmarking status | Four cards: **Job pricings** (percent of active jobs priced and published, including slotted jobs), **Unpriced jobs with employees** (percentage and count of jobs unpriced in a pay market with at least one employee), **Employees in unpriced jobs** (percentage and count of employees whose job code isn't priced in their pay market), and **Employee data freshness** (days since your last employee file upload, with a 90-day limit). Unique job codes are counted, not individual employee and pay market combinations. |
| Quick resume | The six product areas you visited most recently, or six you've pinned. Can't be hidden. |
| Workforce positioning | Up to four of the largest job families by employee count, one row per job family. Markers are positioned relative to the target percentile, which defaults to P50. |
| Survey utilization | Active survey count, unique job codes count, data cuts count, and the last load date, targeting 365 days. Links to the **Surveys** page through **Manage surveys**. |
| Reports | Two tabs: **My favorites** (from the **Favorites** section of **Smart reporting**) and **Recent** (the most recently added and created smart reports for your site). Each card shows the report name and date created. Links to **Smart reporting** through **Browse reports**. |
